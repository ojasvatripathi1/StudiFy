Problem Statement

Why does conventional studying fail?

😴
Zero Engagement
Textbook reading offers no interactivity, feedback, or dopamine rewards — leading to passive, low-retention studying.
📉
No Progress Visibility
Students cannot measure improvement objectively. Absence of analytics makes blind spots invisible.
🤖
Generic Content
One-size-fits-all quizzes fail to adapt to individual pace, topics, or knowledge gaps.
⏰
No Accountability
Without reminders, streak systems, or competition, students lack the consistency to build strong habits.
🏝️
Isolation
No community, leaderboard, or peer benchmark to motivate competitive growth.
✅ StudiFy addresses all of these
● AI-personalised quizzes & study paths
● Gamified streaks, coins & leaderboards
● Real-time productivity analytics
● Always-available AI tutor chatbot

Overview

What is StudiFy?

StudiFy is a full-stack, AI-driven educational platform built as an MCA Final Year Project. It fuses Generative AI with Behavioural Gamification to transform passive studying into an engaging habit loop.

Inspired by the Octalysis Framework, it targets 4 human drives:
Development & Accomplishment — Levels, coins, badges
Empowerment of Creativity — Personalised AI study paths
Social Influence — Competitive leaderboards
Loss & Avoidance — Streak maintenance & daily puzzles

Core Features · 1 of 2

AI Intelligence & Gamification Engine

🧠 AI Study Intelligence
💬
AI Study Assistant Chatbot
Powered by Groq (Llama 3/Mixtral), Google Gemini, or local Ollama. Streams answers via SSE. Stays grounded in uploaded documents, guides without giving away answers.
📝
Smart Quiz Generator
Generates 5-question quizzes from uploaded PDFs or images. A contextual hint system via /api/ai/hint helps without revealing answers.
🔍
AI Study Insights
Analyses focus levels, study duration, and distractions to deliver personalised recommendations and peak performance predictions.
🎮 Gamification Loop
🧩
Daily Puzzle Gated Rewards
Earn coins only by solving a rotating Daily Puzzle:
Memory Game · Math Challenge · Word Scramble — rotates by day of week.
🔥
Streak & Coin Economy
Base: 100 coins. Streak bonus: 100 + (streak × 5) coins. Resets on missed day.
🏆
Leaderboard, Badges & Coin Shop
Global ranking by coins; badge milestones (Quiz Master, 7-Day Streak). Shop sells themes, avatars, boosters tracked in userInventory.

Core Features · 2 of 2

Productivity, Analytics & Document Processing

⏱️
Focused Study Timer
Pomodoro-style session tracking. Users pick a subject & goal. Timer logs duration automatically. Focus rated post-session.
📊
Growth Story Analytics
Powered by Recharts:
Growth Velocity · Engagement Spectrum · Revenue Ecosystem pie chart.
📁
File Processing & OCR
PDF, TXT, Images supported. Tesseract.js OCR client-side. pdfjs-dist extracts text for AI.
☁️
Cloud Image Storage
Cloudinary handles upload, optimisation, and CDN delivery of avatars and profile images.
📅
Daily Quiz System
A unique quiz per day via /api/daily-quiz. Coins awarded based on performance score.
📧
Transactional Email
SendGrid is integrated for automated email delivery — email verification on signup.
